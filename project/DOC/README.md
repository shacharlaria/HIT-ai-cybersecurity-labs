## 📌 Overview & Problem Statement

Modern enterprise Security Operations Centers (SOCs) operate under constant pressure from two major operational bottlenecks:
1. **Alert Fatigue:** High volumes of alerts generated by rigid, deterministic rules overwhelm security analysts with false positives, degrading response times and causing critical threats to be missed.
2. **Evasion & Zero-Day Vectors:** Traditional signature-based detection mechanisms are inherently blind to novel attack techniques, polymorphic behaviors, and stealthy low-and-slow reconnaissance sweeps.

The **Hybrid SOC Threat Detector** addresses these challenges by deploying a fully containerized, two-tiered detection pipeline:
* **Deterministic Layer:** Rapid pattern-matching engine designed to catch known attack signatures, credential abuse attempts, and malformed traffic with minimal latency.
* **Unsupervised Machine Learning Layer (Isolation Forest):** Isolates behavioral anomalies and statistical outliers in high-dimensional network connection telemetry without requiring pre-labeled training corpora.
* **Dynamic CTI Enrichment:** Real-time parsing and resolution of flagged anomalies against the **MITRE ATT&CK STIX 2.1** framework, transforming raw anomaly scores into structured, actionable threat intelligence.
